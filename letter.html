WORDS FROM MY HEART

For the one who makes life beautiful

❀

My dearest Melody,

There are so many things I wish I could put into words, but somehow, even a thousand pages would never be enough to explain what you mean to me. You have become one of the most beautiful parts of my world. Your presence brings a kind of warmth that ordinary days cannot give, and even the smallest moments feel special when they have you in them.

I don't promise that every day will be perfect. I promise something more honest: to keep choosing us, to listen when you need to be heard, to stand beside you through the ordinary days and the difficult ones, and to keep finding new reasons to make you smile. I want to celebrate your happiness, support your dreams, and build a future where we both feel safe, valued, and loved.

Thank you for being you, Melody. If our story is a song, I hope we never rush to the ending. I want all the little verses too — the laughter, the quiet evenings, the silly conversations, the adventures, and all the memories still waiting for us.

With all my love, Always yours ♡
